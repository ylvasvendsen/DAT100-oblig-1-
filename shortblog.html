Introduction to Short Selling

Short sellers are often longs best friends. They spike up stocks far above what they are worth! I thank them repeatedly for enriching me and several of my top trading challenge students.

Short selling is one of my favorite ways to make money in the stock market.

If you are fairly new to trading, the notion of short selling may seem particularly daunting, or even downright scary because of the potentially unlimited liability risk.

But short-selling is not something to be feared. It is a competitive tool you can learn and use to help round out your trading activities and take advantage of situations when you are better off betting on a decline in price.

Important risk warning:

Short selling involves significant financial risk. Because a stock's price can rise without a fixed limit, potential losses can exceed your initial investment. Always understand the risks before trading.

However, I do not recommend shorting stock just because you have the power to do so, or because you think I am telling you to short everything that comes your way. Far from it.

Instead, you need to master the concept of shorting a stock and learn to recognize repeated patterns that could help you forecast price movement.

Without a stop-loss strategy and proper risk management, you are exposing yourself to potentially significant losses.
